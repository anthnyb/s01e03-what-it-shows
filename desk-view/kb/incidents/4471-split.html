  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s 7 April becomes 4 July. Every channel, every tenant. This is the fix that is queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants’ people type month first, as told, and the form reads the date day first. Only the form channel on booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it; the hint and the form’s reading have to agree with the tenant’s locale.
  • shift: the date moves one day earlier, not a swap: “Tuesday 12 May” arrives as “Monday 11 May”, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday. It depends on the recipient’s time zone, not the tenant’s date order. The desk noted these reports as one-day moves, and the #4471 table still counts them under parser.

How the split was made

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports across pages, so they are counted once.
  • parser and hint come from causeOf in #lib/kb/causes: tenant locale, booking form and channel. Intake’s tag is not used: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant often.
  • shift comes from the desk’s own reading (oneDayMove in desk notes). Those reports are taken out of parser because their dates do not swap.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line. Every one of these problems produces the same subjects: “wrong day”, “date is wrong”. Matching on subject could not tell them apart.
